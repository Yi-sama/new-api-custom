- Replaced the pool-wide headline ratio with an always-visible group table.
  Each active group in the selected channels/period has its own configured
  rate, charge, attributed Sub usage, charge/Sub ratio, estimated break-even
  rate, and status. The aggregate comparison remains collapsed.
- Backend scope: `model/profitability.go`, `model/profitability_group.go`,
  and `model/profitability_group_test.go`. Frontend scope:
  `web/src/features/dashboard/components/profitability/` and English/Chinese
  locale entries. Existing unrelated worktree changes were retained.
- Break-even uses allocated purchase cost divided by the sum of each call's
  income basis divided by its historical applied rate. Current configured
  rates are displayed separately and never used to reconstruct old calls.
  The configured value includes the existing group addition; explicit zero
  is preserved and absent configuration is unknown.
- Purchase cost is allocated once by group Sub usage within each channel.
  The all-call estimate assumes all charges are paid. Customer-only excludes
  administrator and gift income but does not remove allocated cost or
  gift-funded Sub consumption. Operating profit remains a separate paid
  customer income minus purchase cost calculation.
- Missing historical rates, pricing overrides, incomplete attribution,
  missing/unallocatable procurement, zero usage, and cash mode do not invent
  a break-even rate. Administrator-only groups are included. Known usage in
  unselected channels does not inflate a selected group's denominator.
- Fixed duplicate accessible region names, async fixture assertions, stale
  break-even values under incomplete status, and misleading status labels.
  The table has keyboard-focusable local horizontal scrolling and bounded
  wrapping for long group names and large numeric values.
